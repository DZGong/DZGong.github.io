Cokecan

An ultra-high-throughput imaging platform for all-optical electrophysiology.

learn more ▼

High-throughput platform for optical electrophysiology

  • 2.3× magnification, 0.5 NA
  • Single-cell resolution (~2.8 µm/pixel)
  • DMD-patterned illumination
  • 1 kHz frame rate for 6×6 mm at 8-bit depth
  • ~90% excitation uniformity across 6 mm
  • Three-color excitation (red, blue, UV)

Screening thousands of cells

The high-throughput design and patterned illumination enable Cokecan to perform perturb-and-observe measurements, screening thousands of cells simultaneously.
